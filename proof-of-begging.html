PROOF OF BEGGING

how the daily begging cycle actually works

Proof of Begging is an endless 24-hour cycle built around the oldest mechanism in crypto: asking strangers for money. Every day, a new Solana meme coin is selected by the community and handed to our Board of Beggars, who spend the next 24 hours relentlessly begging the internet to buy it.

1

The community picks a Solana meme coin. That coin becomes today's target for the next 24 hours.

2

The Board of Beggars begs the internet, non-stop, to buy it. Every SOL raised during the cycle flows into the Proof of Begging treasury.

3

When the clock runs out, 67% of the treasury is used to buy the day's coin, then burned. The other 33% is split equally among the beggars.

4

Everything resets. A new coin is chosen, and the begging begins again.

THE BURN

67%

of the treasury buys the day's chosen coin and burns it, permanently, every single cycle.

THE BEGGARS

33%

is split equally among every beggar on the Board, paid out automatically the moment the cycle ends.

The biggest donors of the cycle are crowned Begging Whales and get a spot on the whale board for that cycle. Every completed cycle, including its coin, its treasury total, its burn, and its payouts, is recorded permanently on the Cycle Log.
